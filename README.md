Global Shoe Sales and Profitability Analysis 2025


Overview

This project uses Tableau to analyze footwear sales performance and profitability for 2025. The analysis examines revenue trends, product demand, brand performance, pricing segments, geographical revenue and sales channels.

The goal is to turn sales data into meaningful business insights that can support commercial planning and performance monitoring.


Business Objectives

. Evaluate total revenue, total profit and units sold.

. Analyze monthly revenue performance.

. Compare revenue across countries and brands.

. Investigate product and shoe-type performance.

. Examine pricing segments and sales volume.

. Compare retail and online revenue.

. Evaluate profitability and profit margins.

. Present the findings in a Tableau dashboard.


Dataset

The dataset contains sales-related fields including:

Sale_ID, Date, Brand, Shoe_Type, Color, Country, Sales_Channel, Customer_Type, Price_USD, Units_Sold, Price_Range, Sales_Performance, Revenue_Category, Revenue_USD, and Estimated Profit.

Tools and Techniques


Tool: Tableau


Techniques: KPI analysis, calculated fields, descriptive analytics, monthly trend analysis, category comparisons, geographical analysis, channel comparison, profitability analysis and dashboard development.


Key Findings


Highest recorded monthly revenue - October: $146,726.46

Lowest recorded monthly revenue - September: $95,768.75

Leading brand by recorded revenue - Skechers: $269,168.63

Retail revenue - $506,125.13

Online revenue - $467,772.32

Retail revenue advantage - $38,352.81


Dashboard

The Tableau workbook includes KPI views for total revenue, total profit and total units sold, together with worksheets for monthly performance, country revenue, footwear demand, price ranges, sales volume, profit margins, shoe-type profitability, sales channels, categories and leading brands.

Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the drivers of monthly revenue variation.

2. Evaluate brand contribution alongside profit margin.

3. Compare retail and online performance using operating costs and profitability.

4. Improve pricing and inventory decisions using product-level demand.

5. Monitor revenue, profit and units sold consistently.

6. Extend the analysis with cost, inventory and historical sales data.

7. Limitations and Further Analysis


Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Tableau can be used to transform retail sales data into a dashboard that supports business performance analysis and evidence-based recommendations.
